🩺 When to call a vet

Pets often hide illness. Trust sudden changes, even when an animal looks cute and calm.

  • 🚨Get urgent veterinary help for struggling to breathe, collapse, seizures, severe bleeding, suspected poisoning, or a swollen painful belly.
  • 👀Arrange a prompt check for not eating/drinking, repeated vomiting, unusual hiding, limping, eye changes or a sudden behavior shift.
  • 🤍Never give human medicine or force food/water without professional advice. For livestock, use a qualified large-animal veterinarian.
